# About scenario.generator

scenario.generator is a browser-based workspace for creating, inspecting,
measuring, and exporting driving scenarios. It supports trajectory-based
scenario design as well as the creation and modification of OpenDRIVE maps.
Scenarios can be enriched with actors, controllers, environment information,
metrics, and postprocessing steps before they are exported for simulation,
analysis, or further processing.

## Contributors welcome!

scenario.generator is open source, and contributions are more than welcome! You can find the source code, report issues, and contribute via
[https://github.com/ika-rwth-aachen/scenario-generator](https://github.com/ika-rwth-aachen/scenario-generator).
